How to delete your MLKD Survivor data

Last updated: October 12, 2026. This is a translation; if there is any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.

1. Data stored only on your phone

The game's progress, settings and queues are stored on the device itself. To delete them, uninstall MLKD Survivor or, on Android, open Settings, Apps, MLKD Survivor, Storage and tap Clear data.

2. Data stored on our server

Send an email to weslleyfeltrin@gmail.com with the subject Excluir dados MLKD Survivor. So we can find your data, include whatever you have:

  • the nickname shown in your Profile and in the Tower ranking;
  • your Google Play Games player name, if you signed in with it;
  • your character's name in MLKD RPG, if you claimed the Spirit of the Fallen King.

You can ask us to delete everything or only part of it (for example, just the ranking). We reply and complete the deletion within 15 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Profile (nickname, portrait and banner) and Tower ranking records;
  • cloud save and the link to your Google Play Games account;
  • the record of your MLKD RPG reward claim;
  • statistics and suggestions sent from your device.

A reward already unlocked on your device stays in your local progress. No data is kept after deletion unless the law requires it: in-app purchase records (purchase code, product, order and date) are kept for the period required by law and for support. Items you have already purchased stay in your progress.

4. Retention periods when you don't request anything

Statistics are deleted after 180 days and suggestions after 12 months. Other data is kept while the account is in use. See the Privacy Policy.
