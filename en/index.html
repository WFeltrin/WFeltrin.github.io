The legend

Valdoria, the broken world

Every adventure in the MLKD Universe takes place in the same land — and begins with the same tragedy.

The Nexus

Before time began, a point of convergence gave birth to six Primordial Elements: Blood, Poison, Fire, Ice, Lightning and Holy Light.

The Curse

From the clash between the elements rose a seventh force, one that corrupts everything it touches.

The MLKD Order

Heroes founded the Order and sealed the elements into Runes to hold back the Curse.

The broken Runes

The Order fell apart, the Runes shattered and each one corrupted a region — from the Cursed Forest to the Throne of Chaos.
