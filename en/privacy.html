MLKD Survivor Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026. This is a translation; if there is any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.

1. Who we are

MLKD Survivor is developed by Weslley Henrique Moreira Feltrin, an independent developer based in Nova Odessa, São Paulo, Brazil, who is the data controller under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018). Privacy contact: weslleyfeltrin@gmail.com.

2. What the game stores on your device

Progress (gold, runes, upgrades, classes, maps beaten, achievements), settings and, if you leave it on, a summary of your latest runs (class, map, result, time, level, kills, the monster that defeated the hero). This data stays on your device only. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location. In Brazil, the game also checks the player's age range on Google Play. It is used only on the device, to apply protections for minors, and is never sent or stored (see section 8).

3. Anonymous statistics and suggestions

To balance the game, the app sends our server a summary of each run (mode, map, class, look, whether you won, duration, level, kills, gold, Tower floor and the monster that defeated the hero), of each ad watched (where, whether the reward was given and, if it failed, the error code), of each time the game is opened, of the game's average speed in each run (frames per second) and of technical errors that happen in the game (only the error message and its place in the code). This data goes with a random device identifier and has no name, email, or Google or Telegram data. You can turn sending off in Settings, Statistics: the queue stored on the device is deleted and nothing else is sent. Statistics are deleted after 180 days.

If you use Send suggestion, the text you write is stored on our server with the device identifier for up to 12 months, only to improve the game. Don't write personal data in the suggestion.

4. Ads

The game shows Google AdMob ads only when you choose to watch them (to revive, draw cards and similar). In Brazil, ads are always non-personalized: we don't use your history to choose what appears, following the rules that protect children and teenagers (Brazil's Digital ECA). Outside Brazil, ads may be personalized by Google, based on the advertising identifier and device activity, according to your choice: in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, this depends on what you choose in the consent window shown when you open the game; in the United States, you can opt out in Settings, Privacy, Ad choices; and in any country you can limit personalization in Android's ad settings (by resetting or deleting your advertising ID). In every case, Google receives technical device data (such as IP address, model and the advertising identifier) to deliver the ad, limit repetition and prevent fraud. This processing follows Google's Privacy Policy.

4.1 Claiming the MLKD RPG reward

If you choose to claim the MLKD RPG reward (the Spirit of the Fallen King), the game creates a random identifier for this device and sends it to our server, together with a single-use code. In MLKD RPG, inside Telegram, you confirm the claim. Our server checks whether your RPG account is eligible (a character at level 10 or higher and not banned) and records the claim.

To make sure each Telegram account claims only once, we store: the device identifier, a scrambled version (hash) of your Telegram ID, which doesn't allow the original number to be recovered, and the date of the claim. We don't store your name, @username, phone number or any other Telegram data. No data, character or item from MLKD RPG is transferred to MLKD Survivor.

This data is kept on our own server, with protected access, for as long as the reward is active in the game. You can ask for deletion through the email in this policy. In that case, the claim record is deleted, but the reward already unlocked on your device stays in your progress. Claiming is optional: if you don't use it, no data is sent to our server.

4.2 Profile and Tower ranking

If you play the Tower of Valdoria, your nickname, the portrait and banner chosen in your Profile, the class used and your records (floor, time and kills) are sent to our server and appear in the ranking, visible to other players. You choose the nickname: don't use your full name or personal data. The game creates a random device identifier to know which records are yours; it isn't shown to anyone.

Offensive nicknames may be changed or hidden from the ranking. You can ask for your records and profile to be deleted through the email in this policy.

4.3 Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game asks Google for a single-use code and our server exchanges that code with Google to find out your player account. We store: your Google Play Games player identifier, your Play Games player name (nickname), which devices are linked to the account and a copy of your game progress (gold, runes, upgrades, classes, maps beaten, achievements and settings), with the date it was saved. We don't receive your email, password or other Google account data.

We use this data only to keep your progress and restore it when you change phones or reinstall the game. If the cloud progress and the phone progress differ, you choose which one to keep. Sign-in is optional: without it, progress stays on the device only. The data is kept while the account is in use; you can ask for deletion through the email in this policy.

4.4 In-app purchases

The game sells Runes, ad removal and item packs. Payment is made through Google Play: we don't receive or store card, bank account or address data. To confirm that a purchase is genuine and deliver the item only once, our server checks with Google and stores: the purchase token, the product, the order number, the date, the random device identifier and, if you signed in with Google Play Games, the player identifier. This data is kept as long as needed for support, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Refunds follow Google Play's rules.

4.5 Performance and advertising measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Since version 61, to know whether the game's advertising brings real players, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records the first launch, app usage (sessions), the end of the first run, the map 1 win, when someone completes 10 runs and purchases (product and amount), together with device identifiers (the app instance identifier and, if available, the advertising identifier). Google processes this data as a service provider, and it is used to measure and improve the game's Google Ads campaigns. We don't send your name, email or progress. You can turn this measurement off in Settings, Statistics: the app stops sending this data. In the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, this measurement follows what you choose in the consent window shown when you open the game, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy, Ad choices. This processing follows Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Legal bases (Brazil)

Performance of the service you asked for (playing, keeping your progress and delivering purchases), legitimate interest (security, fixing errors and non-personalized ads that keep the game free) and your consent (statistics and advertising measurement, which can be turned off, suggestions, the MLKD RPG reward claim and Google Play Games sign-in).

6. Who we share with

With Google AdMob, for ads; with Google Analytics for Firebase, to measure advertising; with Google Play Games, for sign-in (if you use it); and with Google Play, to process purchases. The remaining data (statistics, suggestions, ranking, reward claims and cloud save) stays on our own server, the same one that runs MLKD RPG, in a separate database. We don't sell data.

7. How long we keep it

Data on the device stays until you delete the game or its data. On the server: statistics for up to 180 days; suggestions for up to 12 months; profile, ranking, reward claims and cloud save while the account is in use, or until you ask for deletion; purchase records for as long as required by law and for support.

8. For parents and guardians

This section explains, in plain language, how MLKD Survivor protects children and teenagers and what parents and guardians can do. If you are under 18, it is for you too.

Who the game is for. MLKD Survivor is a pixel art action game with short runs, made for players aged 13 and up. Its content rating on Google Play in Brazil is "Livre" (suitable for all ages). Even so, we know children may play, so the protections below apply to everyone under 18.

Age range from Google Play. In Brazil, the game asks Google Play for the player's age range (for example, "13 to 15"). A window from Google Play itself may appear asking to share this information. If the player declines, the game doesn't ask again for 30 days. The age range is used only on the phone and only to protect: it is never sent to our server, to Google Analytics or to advertisers, is not stored in the game progress and is never used to show personalized ads. When the game doesn't receive the age range, it applies the ad protections for minors.

What changes for players under 18:

  • Ads. They only appear when the player chooses to watch one to earn a reward (for example, to revive during a run). In Brazil, ads are never personalized for anyone. For minors, and for those who haven't shared their age, Google also receives an indication that the player is a minor: this blocks remarketing (ads based on what the person did before) and sending data to third-party measurement companies, and filters ads unsuitable for their age. For those aged 12 or under, the filter is the strictest ("for all audiences").
  • Community group. For those aged 12 or under, the button to join the MLKD group on Telegram (an open chat with adults) does not appear.

What the game doesn't have:

  • No chat and no messaging between players.
  • No loot boxes for sale: nothing random is sold for money.
  • It doesn't ask for name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

Tower ranking. The nickname, portrait and records chosen in the Profile are visible to other players. Advise the player not to use their real name. Offensive nicknames may be changed or hidden. Nobody can send messages through the ranking.

Telegram. Claiming the MLKD RPG reward (the Spirit of the Fallen King) opens MLKD RPG on Telegram, a messaging app with its own rules. The game only opens the conversation with the MLKD RPG bot, not a group. If you don't want the player to use Telegram, you can block its installation with Google Family Link.

Purchases. The game sells Runes, ad removal and item packs, always for real money and through Google Play. To avoid purchases without your permission:

  • on Google Play, under Settings › Authentication, require a password or biometrics for every purchase;
  • if the player has an account supervised with Google Family Link, you can require your approval for each purchase;
  • refunds for purchases made by mistake can be requested through Google Play, under its rules.

Screen time. The game's reminders can be turned off in Settings. Runs on the maps last from 8 to about 20 minutes; the Tower has no time limit, and the player can end the climb whenever they want. With Google Family Link you can set a daily time limit for the game, see how long it was used and block the app at certain times.

Data. What the game collects, why and for how long is in sections 2 to 7 of this policy. Parents and guardians can request access to or deletion of the player's data by emailing weslleyfeltrin@gmail.com or through the Delete data page. Turning off Settings › Statistics stops sending statistics and advertising measurement.

Risks and how we reduce them. The main risks we identified are: purchases without permission (reduced by Google Play and Family Link approval), excessive use (reduced by short runs, notifications that can be turned off in Settings and Family Link limits), contact with strangers (the game has no chat and hides the Telegram group for children) and targeted advertising (prohibited in Brazil for all of the game's players). We review these measures whenever the game changes.

Questions or problems. Write to weslleyfeltrin@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

9. Your rights

You can ask for confirmation, access, correction, deletion and information about sharing through the email above. See how to delete your data. You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

9.1 If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), or your country's equivalent law, also applies to you. The controller is the one named in item 1, with the same contact email.

Legal bases (Article 6 GDPR): performance of a contract (playing, keeping your progress, the ranking, the cloud save and delivering purchases); legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention, fixing errors, anonymous statistics to balance the game and non-personalized ads, when you don't consent to personalization, that keep the game free); and consent (advertising measurement through Google Analytics for Firebase and the ads, according to the consent window shown when you open the game, as well as suggestions and the MLKD RPG reward claim, which only happen when you choose to use them).

Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing based on legitimate interest (for example, by turning statistics off in Settings, Statistics). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings, Privacy, Ad choices, without affecting what was done before. To exercise these rights, write to the email in item 1; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your country's data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

International transfers: the game's controller is in Brazil and Google processes data on servers in several countries. So your data may be processed outside your country, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the standard contractual clauses adopted by Google.

Age: if you are under your country's minimum age of digital consent (14 in Spain), ask a parent or guardian to choose the options in the consent window.

9.2 If you are in the United States

We don't sell your data. Ads may be personalized by Google. If you live in a state with its own privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of data for ads in Settings, Privacy, Ad choices.

10. Security

We keep only what is necessary and use the security mechanisms of Android and Google services.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll let you know inside the game and update the date above.
