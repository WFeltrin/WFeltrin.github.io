The Tower of Valdoria

A corridor that only goes up, while the darkness rises from below. Ten elemental zones, floor traps, a mini-boss every 5 floors and a boss every 10. Between zones, the Wanderer sells potions, relics and a second chance.

Made it to the top? The cycle starts over, stronger. Your highest floor goes on the weekly leaderboard.
